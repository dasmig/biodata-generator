The demographic data in the resources/ directory is derived from:

1. NCD Risk Factor Collaboration (NCD-RisC)
   (https://ncdrisc.org/)
   via Our World in Data (https://ourworldindata.org/)
   Height means and standard deviations by country and sex.
   Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

2. World Health Organization Global Health Observatory
   (https://www.who.int/data/gho)
   via Our World in Data (https://ourworldindata.org/)
   BMI means by country and sex.
   Used under WHO terms of use.

3. Published population studies (Wikipedia compilation)
   Blood type frequency distributions by country.
   Source data is in the public domain.

4. Katsara & Nothnagel 2019 + regional estimates
   Eye colour frequency distributions.

5. VISAGE consortium + regional estimates
   Hair colour frequency distributions.

6. WHO UV guidance + ethnic composition estimates
   Skin type (Fitzpatrick scale) distributions.

7. Papadatou-Pastou et al. 2020 meta-analysis
   Handedness frequency distributions.

You are free to share and adapt the data for any purpose, including
commercial use, provided you give appropriate credit to the original sources.
